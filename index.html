About

I'm Moffat Masila, a graphic designer and social media manager based in Kenya. My work sits at the intersection of design and community — most of it made for scouting groups, youth organisations, and community programmes that need clear, credible visual communication on a real deadline.

Outside of design, I'm an active Rover Scout, which is where most of the posters and logos in this portfolio come from: real events, real trainings, real organisations that needed something made and posted.
